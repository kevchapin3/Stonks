# Large-cap universe

`largecaps.txt`: 295 US large caps on NYSE or Nasdaq from a [Bigdata.com](https://bigdata.com)
company screen run on 2026-11-19 (price >= $10, actively trading, no ETFs or funds).
Preferred shares, near-untraded share classes and a duplicate listing were removed.

Sampled as the 100 largest companies in each of three bands, so it is a sample
and not the full large-cap universe:

| Market-cap band | Tickers |
|---|---|
| $50B and up | ~100 largest (about $114B+) |
| $20B-$50B | ~100 largest in band (about $30B-$50B) |
| $10B-$20B | ~100 largest in band (about $14B-$20B) |

Use the S&P 500 (`^GSPC`) as the market filter for this list.
